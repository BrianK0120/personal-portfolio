Experience

Trackside Engineering Placement - Red Bull Powertrains

At Red Bull Powertrains, I worked on Red Bull's first F1 power unit for the 2026 season as a Trackside Engineering Placement. My work was split across two sub-teams.

Product Validation & Data Science

Product Validation works to model and analyze the power unit to support on-track running, using telemetry and simulation to validate performance and support trackside decisions. My work centered on the following projects:

  • Energy Deployment - Competitor Analysis
  • Seasonal Power Unit Allocation - Genetic Algorithm Optimizer
  • MGU-K Lumped Parameter Thermal Model

Confirmation & Durability

The Confirmation & Durability team is responsible for characterizing power unit performance and reliability through full-life dyno testing, as well as calibrating new engines for pass off ahead of track use. During my time in the team, my responsibilities were the following:

  • V6 Dyno Durability Testing
  • Power Unit Pass-Off
  • Race Ops Rooms Support
  • Power Unit Operational Limits Document
  • FIA Dyno Hours Reporting
