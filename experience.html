Experience

Trackside Engineering Placement - Red Bull Powertrains

July 2025 - July 2026

During my 12-month placement at Red Bull Powertrains, I worked within the Trackside Engineering team supporting the development, validation, and operation of Red Bull's first power unit. My work was split between Product Validation and Confirmation & Durability, giving me experience across both data-driven engineering and power unit testing.

With my team members being the trackside Power Unit Engineers and Performance Engineers on the race team for Red Bull Racing and Visa Cash App Racing Bulls, I have had the chance to work alongside some of the most talented engineers in the sport, gaining direct exposure to how a Formula 1 team operates at the race track.

Product Validation & Data Science

During my time in Product Validation, I worked to model and analyze the power unit to support on-track running, using telemetry and simulation to validate performance and support trackside decisions. Required extensive use of Python and PyTorch, aswell as ATLAS. My contributions were in the following projects:

Energy Deployment - Competitor Analysis

I developed a new competitor analysis methodology, where I reverse-engineered competitor battery energy deployment strategies from onboard audio using FFT and spectrogram analysis. My work determined regions of full K deployment, the start of derating, end of derating, ICE-only power, as well as super K clip harvest vs. lift off harvest. This allowed for comparison against our own deployment profiles, feeding into our lap simulation and energy management strategies.

Seasonal Power Unit Allocation - Genetic Algorithm Optimizer

Built a genetic algorithm optimizer to determine laptime-optimal power unit allocation permutation across a full race season for a given car. Real-life constraints were added to the optimizer, as well as the consideration of introducing addition power units to the pool, with the cost of taking a grid place penalty. The allocation dynamically re-optimizes as races occur, determining the optimal power unit usage for the remainder of the season.

MGU-K Lumped-Parameter Thermal Model

Developed a lumped parameter thermal model of the MGU-K, utilized to proxy temperature on track and to satisfy FIA regulatory requirements for ADUO calculations. The model is deployed on the TAG-700 race ECU, achieving temperature predictions within ±1°C during live running across all four cars. To obtain data for this model, a test specification was written for several hundred dyno transient laps, gathering telemetry across varying MGU-K load, track conditions, and operating conditions.

Confirmation & Durability

In the Confirmation & Durability team, I operated the power unit in V6 dyno test cells for characterizing power unit performance and reliability, as well as calibrating new engines for pass-off ahead of track use. Utilized ATLAS for live data visualization and SystemMonitor for project setup, calibrations, and monitoring the power unit.

V6 Dyno Running

  • Durability Testing: Ran race weekend simulations across varying lap types and operating conditions, characterizing power unit performance and operational limits, identifying faults, and proving out calibrations. Worked with designers to resolve issues and with dyno technicians to run the engines.
  • Pass-Off: Ran new power units on dyno ahead of track use, breaking in with bearing cycles, validating performance using power curves, and calibrating injectors to maximize performance.

Other Responsibilities

  • Race Ops Rooms Support: Supporting trackside power unit engineers for Red Bull Racing and Visa Cash App Racing Bulls during race weekends, monitoring live data on ATLAS, noting faults, collecting oil sample data, monitoring wear metals, investigating issues, and comparing with previous track/dyno running.
  • Power Unit Operational Limits Document: Owned the operational limits document for the power unit, defining safe operating boundaries used by factory and trackside teams. Limits were added and values were updated with dyno and track proveout, with new releases communicated to the race team on a race by race basis.
  • FIA Dyno Hours Reporting: Created dyno hour reports across each period, used for legality checks by the FIA. In tandem, developed a dyno hours monitoring dashboard, processing dyno telemetry automatically through a Docker application hosted on Kubernetes, calculating Operational and Occupancy hours and other statistics to view on a visual dashboard, updating live.
